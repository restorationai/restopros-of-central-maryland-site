## Why Columbia Properties See Water Damage Issues

Columbia was built out in stages starting in the mid-1960s under James Rouse's village concept, and neighborhoods like Wilde Lake and Oakland Mills still carry a lot of that original housing stock. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Columbia

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mount and portable pumps before it has a chance to wick further into subfloor, baseboards, and framing. From there our technicians map moisture with meters and thermal imaging to find out how far the water traveled, which matters in split-level homes where water from an upstairs leak can travel down through floor joists into a finished basement ceiling before anyone notices. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed and monitored daily until materials read dry, not just dry to the touch. Older plaster or paneled basement walls common in Columbia's original village homes hold moisture longer than modern drywall, so we adjust drying timelines rather than pulling equipment early just because the surface looks fine.
