Much of Bethesda's housing stock predates modern waterproofing standards, and the area's proximity to Rock Creek and its tributaries means basements and lower levels are prone to seepage during heavy spring and summer storms. ## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Bethesda

The work starts with a room-by-room inventory, photographed and logged before anything leaves the property. That documentation matters for the insurance claim and it protects the homeowner if there's ever a question about what was damaged versus what was simply moved. From there, items are sorted: some can be cleaned and dried on-site, some need specialized treatment off-site (upholstery, electronics, documents, textiles), and anything unsalvageable is documented and set aside rather than discarded without the homeowner's sign-off. Cleanable items are transported to secure storage until the structural drying and any rebuild work is finished, then returned and placed back in the home. Drying and cleaning methods follow IICRC guidelines, and our team includes IICRC WRT-trained technicians for the water-related side of a loss, since contents jobs frequently start as water damage claims before they become a contents question.
