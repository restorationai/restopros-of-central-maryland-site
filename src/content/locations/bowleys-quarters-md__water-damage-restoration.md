## Why Bowleys Quarters Properties See Water Damage Issues

Bowleys Quarters sits on a peninsula shaped by tidal creeks and the Middle River, and that geography shows up in how water damage develops here. Many homes were built with crawl space foundations rather than full basements, since a high water table makes basements impractical this close to the water. Those crawl spaces trap moisture after storms and plumbing leaks, and because they're often out of sight, a slow leak can soak floor joists and subfloor for weeks before a homeowner notices soft spots or a musty smell upstairs. Older waterfront cottages and additions built before modern vapor barrier codes are especially prone to this, and storm surge or wind-driven rain during a nor'easter can push water in around doors, windows, and roof flashing on homes that sit close to the shoreline.

A lot of water damage in waterfront communities like Bowleys Quarters falls into a gray area between standard homeowner's insurance and NFIP flood coverage, and the cause of the water often determines which policy applies.
