## Why Columbia Properties See Odor Problems That Won't Quit

Columbia's housing stock runs the gamut from split-levels and townhomes built during the planned-community boom of the 1960s and 70s to newer construction closer to Town Center. Older homes in that first wave often have original ductwork and plenty of carpet-over-plywood subfloor, both of which hold onto smoke and pet odor far longer than modern materials. Howard County's humid Mid-Atlantic summers compound the problem: odor-causing compounds bind more aggressively to surfaces in high humidity, and a musty basement smell that shows up every July is frequently moisture working on wood framing and HVAC components rather than anything a simple scrub will fix.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Columbia

We start by finding the source, not just the smell. That means pulling back carpet edges, checking behind baseboards, and in fire-related jobs, inspecting duct interiors and wall cavities where smoke residue settles. Once the source material is cleaned or removed, we match the treatment to the job: thermal fogging for smoke and fire odor that has penetrated porous surfaces, hydroxyl generators for occupied spaces where people or pets need to stay on-site during treatment, and ozone treatment for vacant properties where we can seal the space and run a deeper cycle overnight. Ozone is never run in a home with people, pets, or plants present, so we schedule those treatments around when the property can be fully vacated.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Hydroxyl generators are our default choice for Columbia's townhome communities, where shared walls and tight lot lines mean we can't always guarantee full isolation of the treated unit. Thermal fogging works well on structural smoke odor in single-family homes with more separation from neighboring units. For basement and crawlspace musk, which we see often in Columbia's older split-levels with below-grade family rooms, we pair deodorization with moisture control, since treating the odor without addressing humidity just means it returns within weeks.

## Columbia Insurance & HOA Coordination

Many Columbia neighborhoods fall under community association rules that govern exterior work, dumpster placement, and contractor parking, which matters when a job calls for fogging equipment or ozone generators staged outside. We coordinate scheduling around those restrictions and document the work with before-and-after readings so it supports an insurance claim when the odor stems from a covered loss like a fire or a failed sump pump. For straightforward deodorization jobs unrelated to a claim, we keep the same documentation habit so you have a clear record if the issue resurfaces.

## Local note

Basements in Columbia's older sections frequently have a block foundation with a slightly below-grade walkout, and in summer those spaces trap humidity that a window unit or portable dehumidifier never fully resolves. We've found that deodorizing a musty basement without first running a few days of mechanical dehumidification just means the hydroxyl treatment has to compete with ongoing moisture, so we often recommend a short drying period before the odor treatment itself for better results.
