## Why Hyattsville Properties See Mold Issues

A lot of Hyattsville's housing stock, from older bungalows near the city center to postwar rowhouses and brick duplexes, was built before modern vapor barriers and sump systems were standard. Combine that with the region's high summer humidity and a water table that sits close to the surface in parts of Prince George's County, and you get crawlspaces and basements that stay damp for days after a hard rain. Add an HVAC system that's been running condensation into a clogged drain pan, or a slow roof leak that's gone unnoticed in an attic, and colonization can start well before anyone smells it. Townhome and condo buildings with shared walls add another wrinkle: moisture migrating from one unit's plumbing wall can surface as a mold problem two doors down.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hyattsville

We start by tracking the moisture source, not just the visible growth, because treating the surface without fixing what fed it means the problem comes back within weeks. Containment goes up before any disturbance begins: negative air pressure, physical barriers, and HEPA filtration keep spores from migrating into bedrooms, ductwork, or an adjoining unit, which matters more in Hyattsville's closer-set rowhouses and townhome rows than it would on a larger suburban lot. Affected porous materials like drywall and carpet padding are typically removed rather than treated, while structural wood and framing get cleaned, treated, and dried following IICRC S520 mold remediation guidelines. Once the space tests dry, we document the work for your insurance file and, where a buyer, lender, or HOA board requires it, coordinate post-remediation verification testing with an independent third party.
