## What Does a Fire Damage Restoration Company Actually Do?

A fire damage restoration company stabilizes the structure, removes soot and smoke residue, treats lingering odor, and documents the loss for your insurance claim, usually in that order. The work rarely starts with cleanup.

The first visit is about stopping further damage: boarding broken windows, tarping a damaged roof section, shutting off power or water if needed, and photographing the loss before anything moves. From there, crews wipe down or HEPA-vacuum soot from walls, ceilings, and hard surfaces, since soot is acidic and keeps etching glass, metal, and finishes the longer it sits. Our [fire damage restoration process guide](/blog/fire-damage-restoration-process/) walks through each phase in more detail.

Odor is its own project. Smoke particles work into drywall, insulation, and fabric, so a wipe-down alone usually isn't enough; most jobs need thermal fogging, ozone treatment, or a hydroxyl generator running for a set period, matched to how deep the smoke penetrated. Salvageable contents, furniture, electronics, clothing, often get cleaned on-site or packed out to a cleaning facility and returned once the space is ready.

## What Should You Ask a Fire Damage Restoration Company Before You Sign?

The right questions tell you more than a star rating does. Ask these before you hire anyone for fire or smoke damage work.

- **Are you available 24/7, or only during business hours?** A fire doesn't wait for 9 a.m.
- **What standard do your technicians follow for fire and smoke work?** Look for a reference to the IICRC S700 standard, not just "we've done this before."
- **Do you handle contents cleaning in house, or is it subcontracted?** Either can work, but you want to know who's actually touching your belongings.
- **Will you document the loss for my insurance adjuster?** Photos, moisture readings, and a written scope protect your claim.
- **Can I get a written scope of work before anything starts?** Verbal estimates leave too much room for surprise invoices.
