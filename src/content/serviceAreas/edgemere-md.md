## Restoration emergencies common in Edgemere

Edgemere sits low against tidal water, which shapes the kind of damage we see. Nor'easters and the remnants of tropical systems push storm surge and heavy rainfall into low-lying yards and crawlspaces, and that water doesn't always recede quickly given the flat grade near the shoreline. Baltimore County's humid summers add a second pressure: once water gets into a wall cavity or subfloor, mold can establish itself within 24 to 48 hours, especially in homes with limited ventilation or older insulation. Winter brings its own risk in the form of frozen and burst pipes in homes with original plumbing that was never upgraded for modern freeze protection. Fire and smoke calls tend to follow aging electrical panels and older heating equipment common in the area's housing stock.
