## Restoration emergencies common in Bowleys Quarters

Bowleys Quarters sits on a narrow peninsula surrounded by Seneca Creek, Hopkins Creek, and the Middle River, which means tidal surge, wind-driven rain, and groundwater intrusion are routine stressors on homes here, not rare events. Nor'easters and the tail end of Atlantic hurricanes push water up the tributaries and over bulkheads, and homes built close to the waterline often see crawlspace and slab flooding even from storms that stay offshore. Between storms, the area's humidity and proximity to brackish water create steady conditions for mold colonization behind baseboards and under flooring once a leak or flood event goes unaddressed for more than a day or two. Older waterfront cottages that were never fully winterized also see frozen and burst supply lines during hard freezes, since many were built as seasonal camps before being converted to full-time residences.

## Services we provide in Bowleys Quarters

Water damage in Bowleys Quarters often involves tidal or storm surge pushing into crawlspaces and ground-floor living space rather than a simple pipe leak, so extraction and drying plans have to account for brackish water, silt intrusion, and the slower drying times that come with homes built over water or marsh fill.
