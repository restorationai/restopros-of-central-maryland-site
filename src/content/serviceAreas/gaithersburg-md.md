## Restoration emergencies common in Gaithersburg

Gaithersburg sits in the I-270 technology corridor, where decades of dense residential growth followed the highway north out of Rockville. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Gaithersburg

Gaithersburg's housing stock spans mid-century ramblers and split-levels near the older sections of the city to townhouse and condo development built from the 1980s onward as the I-270 corridor expanded. Older homes may still carry galvanized supply lines, cast iron drain stacks, or asbestos-containing materials in flooring and pipe insulation, all of which change how a restoration crew plans demolition and disposal. Many properties are slab-on-grade or have shallow crawlspaces rather than full basements, so a slow leak can sit against framing and insulation far longer before anyone notices a sign of it.
