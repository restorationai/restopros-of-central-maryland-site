## Restoration emergencies common in Lochearn

Lochearn sits in a part of Baltimore County where mid-century suburban development meets older drainage infrastructure, and that combination shows up in the calls we get. Heavy summer thunderstorms and the occasional remnant of a tropical system can overwhelm storm drains faster than aging residential sewer laterals can handle, which backs water into basements and crawlspaces. Winter brings freeze-thaw cycles that stress pipes in homes with original plumbing, and a hard freeze followed by a quick thaw is a classic setup for a burst supply line. Humid Maryland summers also mean that any water intrusion, from a slow roof leak to a failed washing machine hose, has a short runway before mold starts colonizing wet drywall and subfloor.
