## What we handle

RestoPros of Central Maryland works with homeowners, landlords, and property managers across Rockville, Silver Spring, Bethesda and the surrounding Central Maryland communities, handling the emergency side of property damage: stopping the water, drying the structure, cleaning up after fire and smoke, and removing mold and contamination. Our crews respond to single-family homes, apartment buildings, condos, and commercial spaces, and we document every step so your insurance claim has what it needs.

## Emergency restoration services

When water, fire, or storm damage hits, timing matters more than almost anything else. We answer 24/7 for water damage restoration, extracting standing water and drying structural materials per the IICRC S500 standard before mold or wood rot sets in. Fire and smoke damage calls bring the same urgency: soot is acidic and keeps etching into finishes the longer it sits, so we move fast to clean, deodorize, and assess what's salvageable. Sewage cleanup covers backups and other contamination situations that need to be handled safely and discreetly. Storm damage, including wind-driven roof and window failures common during Maryland's summer thunderstorm season, rounds out our emergency response lineup, with tarping available to stop further water or weather intrusion.

## Specialty services

Not every call is a flood or a fire. We provide mold remediation for homes with musty odors, past water intrusion, or visible growth in crawlspaces and basements, common in the older housing stock found throughout Montgomery and Prince George's counties. Odor removal addresses lingering smells from smoke, pets, or moisture that cleaning alone hasn't resolved. Contents restoration and storage protects furniture, documents, and belongings while the structure is being dried and cleaned.

## What we don't do

We are a mitigation and cleanup company. We do not offer reconstruction, rebuild work, or general contracting. Once drying is verified and the affected area is clean, we hand over the moisture readings, photos, and scope notes so the repair contractor you choose can start from an accurate record.
