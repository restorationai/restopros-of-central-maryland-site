Restoration work starts with a phone call from someone who didn't expect to need one. A pipe let go behind a wall overnight, a basement took on water during a storm, a kitchen fire left smoke damage through half the house. RestoPros of Central Maryland exists to answer that call and start the work of putting the property back together, documenting every step along the way so the insurance side of things is less of a fight.

We're a restoration company first, which means the day-to-day looks less like a storefront and more like a dispatch board. Calls come in, crews go out, moisture meters and air movers get loaded and unloaded. Some days are a slow drip under a dishwasher. Others are a flooded lower level after a hard rain. Either way, the job is the same: assess what the water, fire, or smoke actually did to the structure, not just what it looks like on the surface, and get ahead of the secondary damage before it sets in.

## What we do

Most of our work falls into two categories: the visible damage and the damage nobody sees until a wall gets opened up. Water that looks dried on the surface can still be sitting in subfloor or wall cavities days later, and that's where mold gets its start. We run the drying process per IICRC S500 standards, which means tracking moisture content in materials over time rather than guessing when a space feels dry. On the fire and smoke side, the work is about more than removing soot, it's addressing the residue and odor that settle into porous materials long after the smoke clears.

A good share of the job is also paperwork. We photograph and document losses the way carriers expect, which helps homeowners avoid disputes over what was actually damaged and what work was necessary. Being on call around the clock means crews are available nights and weekends when most property damage actually happens.

## Our certifications and licensure

Our technicians hold IICRC Water Restoration Technician (WRT) certification, which sets the standard for how water losses are assessed, categorized, and dried. It's not a marketing badge, it's a training standard that covers things like psychrometrics, moisture mapping, and when a material can be dried in place versus when it needs to come out. That matters because the wrong call on either end either prolongs a loss or costs a homeowner materials that didn't need replacing.

## Where we work

We're based in Rockville and work throughout Central Maryland, an area that mixes mid-century brick ramblers, newer construction along the I-270 corridor, and older rowhomes and basements that weren't built with today's storm patterns in mind. Heavy summer thunderstorms and the occasional nor'easter put a lot of local basements to the test, and older plumbing in homes built before modern code updates fails in ways that are predictable once you've seen enough of it. Knowing the housing stock here, not just the general process, is part of what makes the response faster.
