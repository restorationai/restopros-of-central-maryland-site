RestoPros of Central Maryland dispatches crews across the greater Rockville area and the surrounding counties, with phones answered 24/7 for water, fire, mold, and biohazard calls. When you reach out, we gather the basics on the loss, get a crew routed to you, and confirm arrival order based on severity and distance from our Rockville base.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Rockville and responds throughout Montgomery County and into the neighboring communities along the I-270 and I-495 corridors. That footprint covers a mix of housing stock, from older brick colonials near the District line to newer construction further out, each with its own quirks when water, smoke, or mold get into the structure.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Rockville, including the immediate Montgomery County suburbs, typically see us first simply because of drive time. From there, coverage extends into the broader Central Maryland region, where a crew may be routed after a closer job wraps or depending on how the morning's call volume shakes out. At the outer edge of our service area, expect slightly longer lead times, especially during rush hour on 270 or 495, or after a regional storm event pulls multiple trucks into the field at once. We don't promise a specific arrival window because traffic and loss severity both move that number, but we do prioritize active water intrusion, fire damage, and biohazard calls over scheduled inspections.

## Don't see your city?

If your address isn't listed among our service area pages, call anyway. Coverage areas shift based on crew availability and the size of the loss, and for larger water, fire, or biohazard jobs we will often travel outside our usual radius. The fastest way to find out if we can get to you is a phone call, not a guess based on a map.
